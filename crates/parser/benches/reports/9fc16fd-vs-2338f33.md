# Parser Benchmark Comparison

Comparing baseline `9fc16fd` against current `2338f33`.

- Baseline: `9fc16fd`
- Current: `2338f33`
- Environment: Linux `aarch64` container with Rust, `cargo-criterion`, Valgrind, and `gungraun-runner` baked into the image
- Suites: Criterion wall-clock + Gungraun Callgrind/DHAT

## Conclusions

- From a performance standpoint, this is a decisive net win. Across the full Criterion suite, the sum of representative runtimes fell from **8.103 ms** to **3.725 ms**, a **-54.03%** reduction. That is not a marginal tuning pass; it is a substantial step change in how quickly this parser moves work.
- The strongest gains landed in the paths people actually feel first. The combined parse pipeline (`parse_document` + `build_tree` + `parse_end_to_end`) dropped from **5.656 ms** to **3.504 ms** (**-38.05%**). Rendering fell even harder, from **2.436 ms** to **207.530 us** (**-91.48%**), and reference resolution moved from **1.662 us** to **991.726 ns** (**-40.34%**).
- Memory tells the same overall story. Aggregate DHAT allocated bytes across the measured suite fell from **13.2 MB** to **9.6 MB** (**-27.38%**). Aggregate peak live heap fell from **5.4 MB** to **4.1 MB** (**-24.00%**), and the single worst peak dropped from **1.1 MB** to **871.0 KB** (**-24.83%**).
- The remaining regressions are real, but they are concentrated rather than systemic. Tiny lookup helpers regressed from **33.219 ns** to **52.569 ns** (**+58.25%**), and `extract_text` moved from **8.065 us** to **11.679 us** (**+44.81%**). Those should be treated as the next focused cleanup pass, not as evidence that the broader refactor missed the mark.

- Criterion: 14 improved, 8 regressed. Biggest win: `render_markdown/plain/boe-a-2021-13171` (-95.08%). Biggest regression: `lookup_path/boe-a-1978-31229` (+128.64%)
- Callgrind (`Ir`): 18 improved, 4 regressed. Biggest win: `render_markdown/plain/boe-a-2021-13171` (-94.67%). Biggest regression: `lookup_anchor/boe-a-2021-13171` (+22.44%)
- DHAT (`TotalBytes`): 18 improved, 4 regressed. Biggest win: `lookup_anchor/boe-a-1978-31229` (-100.00%). Biggest regression: `extract_text/boe-a-2021-13171` (+184.70%)

## Legend

- 🟢 Improved: current beat the baseline
- 🔴 Regressed: current is slower or heavier than the baseline
- ⚪ Flat: less than 1% change
- ⚫ N/A: no comparable metric was available
